I am a Senior Engineer - Platform Security at Qualcomm, Hyderabad, with experience in trusted computing, kernel security, and network automation.

I hold an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master’s degree in Security and Cloud Computing (SECCLO), during which I studied at Aalto University, Finland, and EURECOM, France. My academic and research work focused on confidential computing, remote attestation, eBPF-based monitoring, and secure 5G systems. At Ericsson Nomadic Lab in Finland, I contributed to research on kernel-level attestation, system call tracing, QUIC protocol, and SGX-based secure networking.

Before my master’s, I worked at Cisco, building automation and full-stack solutions for large-scale 5G packet core deployments using Kubernetes and containerized platforms. My foundation in computer science comes from Amrita School of Engineering, Coimbatore, where I developed a lasting interest in systems and security.

I am particularly interested in platform and network security, with a growing focus on applying Artificial Intelligence to practical cybersecurity challenges. I enjoy working on security problems that combine systems depth with real-world impact.
